Welcome to the Square

A community as bold as its address.

Charlotte Square is an upscale urban residence at the heart of Rochester's East End: a block from Main Street and East Avenue, beside the Inner Loop's new green edge, and built around the way you actually live.

Park in the secure garage, lock up your bike, and ride the elevator home without stepping back outside. Upstairs, open floor plans and upscale finishes open onto your own outdoor room: an oversized terrace on the podium level, or a balcony with pocket-park and skyline views above.

ROC the East End

The best of Rochester, on foot.

The East End runs from Main Street east along East Avenue to Alexander Street: theaters, galleries, coffee, dinner, and late nights. Charlotte Square sits right in the middle of it.

Arts & culture

Eastman Theatre, The Little, RoCo, and Eastman School recitals.

Eat & drink

East Ave dining, Java's, Kirkpatrick's, and Alexander St nightlife.

Everyday

Harts Local Grocers, downtown employers, the Inner Loop greenway.

Getting around

RTS transit, I-490 in minutes, and bike lanes from your garage.
