Privacy policy

What we collect and why.

Contact form. When you send a message, we receive the details you enter (name, email, phone, and your message) so we can respond to your inquiry about Charlotte Square. We use it for that purpose only.

Resident portal. Rent payments and maintenance requests are handled through our resident portal, operated by Rent Manager. Its own privacy policy applies to information you enter there.

Maps. Location maps on this site are embedded from Google Maps, which may set cookies according to Google's privacy policy.

No selling, ever. We do not sell or rent your personal information. To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, email tesacoleman9@gmail.com.

Last updated: September 2026. Please have counsel review this policy before launch.

Accessibility

Built for everyone.

Charlotte Square is committed to a website that works for people of all abilities. This site is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines: keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text, sufficient color contrast, visible focus states, and respect for reduced-motion settings.

If anything on this site is difficult to use, please tell us at (585) 245-3071 or tesacoleman9@gmail.com and we will fix it and help you get what you need.
