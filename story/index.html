The Charlotte story

Built for the city. Designed for you.

When a vacant lot on Charlotte Street opened up beside the Inner Loop redevelopment, a block from both Main Street and East Avenue, it was clearly one of the best sites in downtown Rochester. Charlotte Square was built to earn it.

How it came together

Four decisions that make the Square what it is.

01

A block from everything

The East End site sits beside the Inner Loop East transformation, one block from Main Street and East Avenue. That means walkability and bikeability to every amenity, and quick access to the city center, public transit, and I-490.

02

A podium with purpose

Secure parking and bike storage fill the ground floor, with three levels of homes above. You move from car or bike to elevator to home to amenities without ever going back outside. Bikes stay out of the weather and out of your living room.

03

Outdoor rooms, not afterthoughts

Homes on the podium level open onto oversized private terraces, outdoor living rooms overlooking the street. Homes above get large balconies facing the pocket park and the Rochester skyline.

04

A new chapter

Charlotte Square is now managed by Evolution24 Properties, a Rochester-based team with a hands-on, approachable style. Same great building, sharper service.

The spec sheet

LEED Gold certified, and it shows.

LEED is the U.S. Green Building Council’s rating system for green buildings. A building earns points for energy efficiency, water use, indoor air quality, materials and location, and independent reviewers check the work before it is certified. Charlotte Square earned Gold, the second-highest of the four levels.
