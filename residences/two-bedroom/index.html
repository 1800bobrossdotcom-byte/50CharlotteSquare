Good to know

Two-bedroom questions, answered.

How big are the two-bedroom apartments at 50 Charlotte Square?
About 1,100 to 1,400 square feet, with two bedrooms and two full baths. Sizes vary by apartment; the leasing team can share the layout of a particular home.
Can the second bedroom be a home office?
Yes. It is a full bedroom, so it works as an office, a guest room or a bedroom.
Do the two-bedroom apartments have outdoor space?
Yes. Every home at 50 Charlotte Square has private outdoor space: a terrace on Level 2, or a balcony on Levels 3 and 4.
Is parking included?
Yes. One space in the secure podium garage, directly below the homes, comes with every apartment, and there is secure bike storage too. Ask about additional spaces.
What does a two-bedroom apartment rent for?
Rents across the building run $1,750 to $3,695 a month, as of September 2026. For current two-bedroom pricing and availability, call (585) 748-5588 or send a message.
Can I tour a two-bedroom apartment?
Yes. Tours are by appointment and take about thirty minutes; the leasing office is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 4pm.
