Good to know

Questions, answered.

What's included in rent?
Select utilities and one garage parking space are included with every home. Ask the leasing team for the full breakdown for the home you're considering.
Is parking available?
Yes. Charlotte Square's podium design puts secure parking on the ground floor, directly below the homes, along with secure bike storage. One space is included; ask about additional spaces.
Are pets welcome?
Please contact the leasing team for the current pet policy, including any breed, weight, or quantity limits and fees.
How do I apply?
Reach out to schedule a tour or check availability. Applications are handled online, and we'll walk you through every step.
What lease terms are offered?
Lease terms vary by home and season. Ask our team about the options currently available.
Who manages Charlotte Square?
Evolution24 Properties, a Rochester-based management company with a hands-on, approachable style and a commitment to clear communication with residents.
