Using it

Which file to use

PNG

Websites, email signatures, social posts, Word, Google Docs and slides. Transparent backgrounds. Three widths: 600, 1200 and 2400 pixels (the C: 256 to 2048). Pick one at least twice as wide as it will show.

SVG

For web developers and designers. Sharp at any size and the smallest files.

PDF

For printers, sign makers and Illustrator. Sharp at any size. The white versions look blank on a white page; they are there.

For print there is no official Pantone or CMYK match yet. Give the printer #A93B3F and ask for a proof, or hold it against the sign on the building.

Lettering

Instrument Serif Italic

"Charlotte" in regular, "Square" in italic.

DM SANS BOLD

"At the East End", in capitals, spaced out.

Both are free from Google Fonts and are the website's fonts. The logo files don't need them: the lettering is already drawn in.

Give it room

¼ of the C's square on every side

Keep text, edges and other logos at least a quarter of the red square's height away.

Smallest sizes

Horizontal logo: 150 pixels wide on screen, 1.5 inches in print.
Stacked logo: 250 pixels, 2 inches.
The C: 16 pixels, ¼ inch.
Smaller than that, use a short version or the C.

Please don't

  • Stretch, squash, tilt or rotate it.
  • Change the colors, or put the red C on a red or busy background.
  • Move, resize or swap the parts.
  • Add shadows, outlines or effects.
  • Retype the name in another font.
  • Use a screenshot or a copy from the website. Use these files.
