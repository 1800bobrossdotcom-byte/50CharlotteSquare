The terrace

A pergola for shade, a fire pit for October, shared grills for everyone, and the Rochester skyline for free. Set above the street on the podium level, this is where the building gathers.

  • Shared grills

A CrossFit-inspired setup with a rig, free weights, and a cardio row facing the street, so you can train hard without leaving home.

The community room and lobby lounge

A modern lounge with a leather sofa, a kitchen bar for hosting, and a fireplace for winter. Down the hall, the lobby lounge wraps you in reclaimed wood and local art.
